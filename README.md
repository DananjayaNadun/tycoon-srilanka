# 🚗 Tycoon Sri Lanka — A Political Simulation Game

<img width="1983" height="793" alt="Tycoon Sri Lanka" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f068941f-c4f7-4f68-bc69-85ea9e77a5b7" />

It is a **story-driven simulation** inspired by the darker chapters of Sri Lanka’s political history — corruption, power struggles, and systemic control — influenced by real-world revelations like the Pandora Papers.
While the gameplay includes familiar mechanics like **buying, repairing, and selling cars**, those are only the surface.  
Underneath, the game explores how power, money, and influence shape a nation.

---

## 🎯 Core Idea

You start small — managing cars, earning money.

But as you progress, you enter deeper systems:
- Political influence  
- Corruption networks  
- Hidden transactions  
- Public unrest  
- Institutional control  

This is a **tycoon game with consequences**.

---

## 🧩 Game Structure (4 Historical Stages)

The game is divided into four narrative eras:

### 1️⃣ The Seeds of Division (1948–1956)
- Early independence period  
- Policy decisions begin shaping long-term divisions  
- Player learns basic economy + influence systems  

---

### 2️⃣ The Rise of Political Violence (1971 & 1983)
- Political instability increases  
- Risk systems introduced (loss, sabotage, unrest)  

---

### 3️⃣ Institutional "Darkness" (1978 Constitution)
- Power becomes centralized  
- Systems become harder to control  
- Corruption mechanics expand  

---

### 4️⃣ Modern Corruption & The "Aragalaya" (2022)
- Public pressure, protests, economic collapse  
- Player decisions directly affect survival
